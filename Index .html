What is a SIP?

SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It allows an investor to invest a fixed amount regularly, usually every month, in a mutual fund scheme. SIP can help investors develop disciplined investing habits and invest for long-term financial goals.

How does a SIP Calculator work?

A SIP calculator estimates the future value of your regular investments using the monthly investment amount, expected annual return and investment period. The result includes your total invested amount and estimated returns.

Why should you use a SIP Calculator?

A SIP calculator can help you understand how your investment may grow over time. You can change the monthly SIP amount, expected return and investment duration to compare different investment scenarios before planning your investments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SIP investment guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns are market-linked and are not guaranteed. The calculator only provides an estimate.

What is a good SIP amount to start with?

You can start with an amount that comfortably fits your monthly budget. The important thing is to invest regularly according to your financial goals and risk tolerance.

Can I use this SIP Calculator for long-term investments?

Yes. You can calculate estimated SIP values for investment periods from 1 to 50 years.

Can SIP returns be higher or lower than the calculator result?

Yes. Actual returns can be different because mutual fund performance depends on market conditions and other factors.
