What is SIP?

SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It allows investors to invest a fixed amount regularly in a mutual fund scheme.

How does SIP work?

In a monthly SIP, you invest a fixed amount every month. The amount is invested according to the selected mutual fund scheme.

What is compounding?

Compounding means earning returns on your previous investment returns over time. The longer you remain invested, the greater the potential impact of compounding can be.

Is SIP return guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns are market-linked and are not guaranteed. The calculator uses an assumed annual return only for estimation.
